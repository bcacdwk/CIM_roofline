Within each scenario, $\rho$ spans about $10^2$ across the ten configurations, whereas $\tau$ spans more than $10^4$. The streaming paths are constrained by input application, sensing and conversion, and/or digital reconstruction under similar peripheral conditions. Resident updates additionally depend on the storage write mechanism, update granularity and actual write-resource organization. For example, reference SRAM ACIM and NOR provide comparable streaming rates of about 40 and 24\,MB/s, but resident rates of about 3200 and 0.080\,MB/s. Similar evaluation capability therefore need not imply a similar cost of establishing state. The wider variation in update service shifts the balance intensity and the reuse needed to sustain streaming calculation.【这里写的有点啰嗦，但是也挺重要的，看看怎么简化一些吧】

A memory's ability to retain state compactly is distinct from its ability to update that state quickly. Fig.~\ref{fig:hardware-capacities}(b) uses independent binary cell/tile geometry references, combining reported geometry with estimates under explicit layout conditions. The last is a conditional model, not a reported complete cell. NAND uses a 500-layer projection of the published lateral geometry~\cite{NAND-04}. Density and normalized bit footprint express the same geometry in two ways, rather than independently validating it; $F_{\mathrm{mem}}$ uses a nominal node or a directional half-pitch as available. These quantities describe neither complete-chip area nor effective INT8 weight density, and a technology's geometry and throughput references need not be the same configuration. On the workload side, Table~\ref{tab:workload-intensity} selects 72 finite operating points from matrix stages of six fixed models~\cite{qwen35,ministral3,qwen36,hy3,ling,mimo}. These are exact logical counts at one byte per element, obtained from model structure rather than measured execution of complete models. Weight projections and the matrix computations in MLP/FFN or MoE stages follow the one-load, cumulative-use accounting of Section~\ref{sec:model}-C. Composite stages aggregate their actual matrices and count a shared source input once: gate and up projections share an input, while down receives a new input that is counted separately. The load remains part of the window even when the weights are fixed. Consequently, stage structure, input sharing and actual use after loading determine $\SI$; model size or the label ``fixed weights'' alone does not.
