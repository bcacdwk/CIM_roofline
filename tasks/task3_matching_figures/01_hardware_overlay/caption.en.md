Native full-load capability and reuse references. The horizontal axis is resident-update capacity τ; the vertical axis is σ=Nρ, the full-matrix-equivalent streaming capacity for each native INT8 configuration. Large filled markers denote typical service scenarios, and smaller open markers retain the original paired scenarios. Dashed circles summarize only these finite scenarios in equal-scale logarithmic coordinates; their interiors do not denote additional feasible configurations. For one complete matrix load followed by U complete input vectors, balance occurs at σ=Uτ, or U*=Nρ/τ=T_R/Δ_S. Points above a given reference line are resident-bound and points below it are streaming-bound. The four U values come from accepted Table II(a) GEMM W[128,128] states (1K=1024); across different native shapes only the reuse requirement is transferred, rather than claiming an unmodeled deployment of that common matrix. Capability coordinates retain each configuration's resources and service boundary and do not imply equal-area performance or simultaneously attainable route peaks. MB/s denotes 10⁶ Byte/s.
