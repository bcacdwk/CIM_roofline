Logical demand of the selected QKV projection stages under one complete weight load. Black points show the accepted finite reuse states U = 1, 1K, 128K, and 1M for Qwen3.5-2B (including its output gate) and MiMo-V2.5-Pro; Q_S = UD counts the shared stage input once, and Q_R = DN_proj counts all selected weights. The panels share logarithmic byte axes. Colored rays use the typical scenarios of three native 128 × 128 engines and an explicit sequential full-tile component-service reference: m_K = D/128, m_N = N_proj/128, and each of the m_K m_N tiles is loaded once and serves U input fragments. Native input replay is m_N Q_S, giving rho_eff = rho/m_N, tau_eff = tau, and RI*_eff = RI*/m_N = U*/N_proj. A point above (below) a ray has the larger streaming (resident-loading) component service demand. MiMo has 15.9 times the resident bytes and three times the logical input bytes at equal U, while the matched-tile balance threshold U* remains unchanged. Cross-tile reduction, switching, upstream transfers, and output storage are outside this component reference; the minimum of the two route-specific rate limits is an ideal resource upper bound, whereas fully non-overlapping service takes at least the sum of the two component times. This is not an achieved-throughput or deployment claim. Only typical hardware scenarios are shown. U includes the first use; 1K = 1024 and 1M = 1024².
