**Critical reuse for a complete native matrix load.** Each row retains the native configuration and paired service scenarios from Task I. For equal-width INT8 inputs and resident weights, the balancing reuse is $U^*=T_R/\Delta_S=N\rho/\tau$. Larger filled symbols denote typical scenarios; small open and pale symbols denote the original short- and long-time pairs. Connecting segments summarize only the three actual scenario values, not a continuous feasible envelope. Small vertical offsets separate coincident scenario markers without altering $U^*$. Dashed lines show the Table II(a) reuse references $U=1,128,1\mathrm{K},128\mathrm{K}$ for $W[128,128]$; they are transferred reuse-count references for hardware with other native shapes, rather than completed workload mappings. For each native service, $U<U^*$ is resident-bound and $U>U^*$ is streaming-bound. At $U=128$, MRAM is resident-bound for the short and typical pairs but streaming-bound for the long pair. These thresholds describe two-route resource ceilings, not measured throughput or a device ranking. $1\mathrm{K}=1024$, $1\mathrm{M}=1024^2$.
